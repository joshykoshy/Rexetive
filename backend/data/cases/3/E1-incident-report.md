# Incident report: Copperfield Co-op

Written by the co-op board chair, Hana Ellery, 25 September 2026.

On Thursday 24 September the external auditor, Sam Okoro, arrived to start the annual audit and found that the handwritten cash ledger for July to September was missing from the office drawer. A reconciliation against bank statements shows a cash shortfall of 4,800 over those three months. The office has no alarm. Keys: one office key hangs in the shared key cabinet (sign-out sheet), and the treasurer, Lio Marsh, holds a personal key. Volunteer coordinator Petra Quill was the last person to sign out the shared key.
