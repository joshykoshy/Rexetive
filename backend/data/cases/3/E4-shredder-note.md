# Note on shredder bin contents

Prepared by Sam Okoro, 27 September.

The office shredder bin contained cross-cut fragments of lined ledger paper. Reassembled fragments show handwritten column headings "date / till count / deposited" and partial entries for 7, 14 and 21 August. The handwriting on the fragments matches the treasurer's entries in earlier ledgers. The shredder was emptied on Monday 21 September, so the fragments were added after that.
