# Deposit pattern analysis

Prepared by Sam Okoro, 28 September.

From July to September the co-op made cash deposits after every till count, except on Fridays. Of the 13 Fridays, 11 have no deposit. The volunteer rota shows Lio Marsh counted the till on those Fridays. The average Friday till count in the previous year was about 410; 11 missing Friday deposits at that level would total roughly 4,500, close to the 4,800 shortfall.
