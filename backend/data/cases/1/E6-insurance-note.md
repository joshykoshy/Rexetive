# Insurance amendment record

Marrowby Mutual Fine Art policy, amendment dated 22 August 2026.

The insured value of "Tide at Dusk" was raised from 180,000 to 410,000, effective immediately. The amendment was requested and signed by Aron Pell, deputy director, citing "a recent private valuation". No copy of the private valuation was found in the gallery's files. The director, Mira Solberg, says she was not told about the change.
