# Incident report: missing painting

Filed by Officer Dana Reyes, Marrowby Police, 12 September 2026, 23:05.

At 22:48 the gallery director, Mira Solberg, reported that the oil painting "Tide at Dusk" was missing from Storeroom B at the Lantern Street Gallery. The painting had been moved to the storeroom at 20:30 so it could be reframed the next morning. The opening gala was taking place in the main hall, with about 120 guests.

Storeroom B has one door, controlled by a badge reader. There were no signs of forced entry. The building alarm was switched off between 21:30 and 22:00 for scheduled maintenance. The frame was left behind, leaning against the wall; the canvas had been removed from it.

Staff on duty: guard Tomas Brandt, conservator Ilse Varga (left the gala early), deputy director Aron Pell, and two catering staff.
