# Analyst note: badge reader forensic check

Prepared by K. Imre, security systems analyst, 15 September 2026.

The reader's internal diagnostic log shows that the 21:42 and 21:51 events used credential serial 0417 but with a different card UID from the card issued to Ilse Varga. Two different physical cards presented the same serial number. This pattern is consistent with a cloned (duplicate) credential.

The genuine card 0417 was inside Ms Varga's handbag when officers inspected it on 13 September. Card cloners of this type need about one second of close contact with the original card. Staff badges were left in an unlocked tray in the staff kitchen during the gala's preparation, according to two catering staff.
