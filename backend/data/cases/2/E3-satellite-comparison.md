# Analyst note: satellite comparison

Prepared by Dr Haddad, 5 April 2026.

Satellite-derived surface temperature over Halvard Island shows a drop of about 9 degrees between 14 March 01:00 and 06:00, matching the storm seen at the neighbouring stations. The station's raw logger memory card (recovered on 30 March) also contains this drop. The published values for the same hours are flat. Conclusion: the sensor worked correctly; the published file was edited to remove the storm signal.
