# Data integrity report: Halvard Station

Filed by Dr Noor Haddad, data quality lead, 2 April 2026.

During routine review, the hourly air temperature series from Halvard Island for 13 to 15 March 2026 showed almost no variation: values changed by less than 0.1 degrees per hour for 61 consecutive hours. Neighbouring stations recorded a strong winter storm on 14 March. The raw logger file and the published file no longer match. The published file was modified on the station terminal. Staff on the island in March: station lead Rune Halden and technician Bea Lund.
